In Summary

Three items on this list are unfinished: affiliate tracking, the waiting experience during a try on, and brand onboarding. They share one single cause.

The app's foundations were built in a way that makes its parts work against each other, and every attempt at those three ran into the same wall. Finding that wall, and proving it with real testing, is what the last month has been. The rebuild is what finally unblocks all three.

One thing I want to own. When I said brand ready in month two, I planned against how the app looked rather than how it was built underneath. It presented far better than it was. That was my judgement to make and I got it wrong, and I would rather tell you than let the date quietly slip.
